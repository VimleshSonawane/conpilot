The problem

Overruns are visible in the data long before anyone acts on them

Construction teams don’t lack data. They lack data they can trust in time. Errors sit in schedules for weeks, and by the time a monthly report shows the problem, the money is spent.

TodayWith ConPilot

Errors are found late, by hand

A duplicate WBS code or broken link quietly distorts every report built on it.

Every row checked on upload

28 automatic checks across schedule, cost and quantities, ranked by severity with the row and the fix.

Time, cost and quantities live apart

P6 or MS Project for time, spreadsheets for money, AutoCAD for quantities.

One view across all three

Earned value ties progress to cost, and the takeoff check prices quantities against each WBS budget.

Reports look backward

Monthly reports explain what already happened.

Forecasts you can test

Critical path, forecast cost and finish, and what-if sliders for recovery plans.

Slippage creeps in unseen

Each weekly update looks fine on its own.

Week-over-week comparison

What slipped, what was spent without progress, and which problems are new or fixed.

Controls software is costly

Small and mid-size teams can’t justify enterprise licences or a full-time scheduler.

Works on files you already have

Nothing to install. It runs in the browser and your file never leaves your device.

Generic AI doesn’t know your project

Chatbots guess. They don’t know your WBS, your CPI or your critical path.

AI grounded in your numbers

Answers cite WBS codes from your file, write the weekly briefing and draft emails to subs.

How to test it

Try every feature in about a minute

  1. Open the demo

    Click Open the live demo. The Overview page shows KPIs, charts, the critical path and a watch list.

  2. Filter and drill in

    Use the filter panel, click any chart bar to cross-filter, or click a task for details.

  3. Add more data

    On Quantities and Changes, load the sample takeoff and last week’s file.

  4. Test a recovery plan

    On Cost, drag the what-if sliders to see the final cost and finish move.

  5. Export the report

    Click PDF report for a multi-page status report.

Use your own data

Check your own schedule

  • Excel (.xlsx, .xls), CSV or PDF, up to 15 MB. Exports and printouts from Smartsheet, MS Project, Primavera P6 and AutoCAD work
  • Column names can vary. It finds WBS, Task, Start, Finish, % Complete, Budget, Actual Cost, Predecessors and Owner
  • Read in your browser. Your file is never uploaded to a server
  • Only a short summary goes to the AI, and only when you ask
